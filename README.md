# Paperwork

**AI handles the paperwork. You handle the humans.**

Paperwork is an AI skill that builds your personal management second brain. It interviews you about how you manage, then generates a complete system — directories, commands, question banks, and signal frameworks — tailored to your workflow, your tools, and your philosophy.

This isn't a template. It's a conversation that builds a system.

## Quick Start

Point your AI coding agent at this skill:

**Claude Code:**
```bash
claude --skill-url https://raw.githubusercontent.com/nobodyiscertain/paperwork/main/PAPERWORK.md
```

**Or manually:**
1. Download `PAPERWORK.md`
2. Add it as a skill to your agent
3. Start a conversation in an empty directory — Paperwork will take it from there

## What You Get

After a 15-20 minute conversation, Paperwork generates:

- **Directory structure** for every person you manage and key relationships
- **CLAUDE.md** that teaches your AI how you manage
- **Slash commands** personalized to your tools (/prep, /log, /health, /think, and more)
- **Question banks** weighted toward your biggest pain points
- **Signal framework** for catching problems early
- **Feedback guide** adapted to your communication style

## How It Works

Paperwork asks about four things:

1. **Your world** — team size, roles, relationships, company stage
2. **Your rhythm** — 1-on-1 cadence, rituals, review cycles
3. **Your tools** — calendar, notes, task manager, meeting recorder, project tracker
4. **Your philosophy** — what matters to you, what signals you watch for, where you struggle

Then it builds everything. No forms. No configuration files. Just a conversation.

## Who It's For

Managers who:
- Have 5+ direct reports and feel like things fall through the cracks
- Want to remember what happened in last month's 1-on-1
- Dread performance review season
- Use AI tools but haven't set them up for management work
- Want a system but don't want to build one from scratch

## What It's Not

- Not a SaaS platform — it's files on your machine
- Not a template you fill in — it's generated for you
- Not opinionated about how you should manage — it encodes how you *do* manage
- Not a replacement for being a good manager — it handles the paperwork so you can focus on the humans
